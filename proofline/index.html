01 / CAPABILITIES

What Proofline does

Separate claims
from evidence.

Threat actors often use screenshots, sample records, directory listings, filenames, or stolen documents as support for much larger claims.

Structured workspace

Compare every side of the claim.

Proofline gives analysts a structured workspace for traceable review without turning incomplete material into certainty.

  • What the threat actor claimed
  • What material was supplied
  • What victim-side reference data shows
  • What can be corroborated
  • What appears contradictory or incomplete
  • What remains unverified
02 / SEQUENCE

Evidence workflow

From claim
to report.

A deliberate sequence preserves the relationship between the original assertion, supplied material, reference data, and resulting assessment.

01 / CLAIMWhat the threat actor says happened
02 / EVIDENCEThe files, screenshots, records, or other material supplied
03 / REFERENCE DATAVictim-side information approved for comparison
04 / CORRELATIONAgreements, inconsistencies, and gaps
05 / FINDINGA traceable assessment of what is supported
06 / REPORTA careful summary for response stakeholders

Claim → Evidence → Reference Data → Correlation → Finding → Report

THREAT-ACTOR CLAIM

Evidence supplied

  • Screenshot of a directory
  • Fifty-row record sample

Authorized reference comparison

  • Several sample identifiers match a historical customer export
  • Directory names are consistent with one known system
  • The supplied material does not establish when the archive was created
  • The evidence does not prove the claimed record count
  • Successful exfiltration of the full dataset remains unverified

04 / OUTPUT

Public-safe review output

Evidence that can be followed back to the claim.

Proofline keeps the assessment legible without publishing unsupported format or export claims.

01

Claim record

The original assertion and its stated scope remain connected to the review.

02

Evidence register

Supplied artifacts are organized with source and context information.

03

Correlation findings

Agreements, contradictions, incomplete support, and unresolved gaps are documented.

04

Controlled summary

Findings can be communicated to authorized response stakeholders without presenting uncertainty as fact.

05B / BOUNDARIES

Product boundaries

Evidence verification.
Clearly scoped.

Proofline is an analyst workspace. It is not an automated verdict engine or a replacement for professional response disciplines.

Proofline does not

Extend beyond the evidence.

  • Negotiate with threat actors
  • Scrape criminal leak sites
  • Attribute attacks
  • Replace forensic investigations
  • Provide legal conclusions
  • Automatically declare an entire extortion claim true or false

Analyst responsibility

The response team makes the final call.

Proofline organizes the review and makes its basis traceable. The response team remains responsible for final interpretation and decisions.
