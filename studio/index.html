02 / Working principles

Build the record alongside the result.

  • Evidence before assertion: distinguish controlled proof, deterministic validation, synthetic capacity, and customer production evidence.
  • Bounded claims: state what a system currently does, what it does not do, and where human judgment remains necessary.
  • Operational clarity: design around real workflows, authority, dependencies, and failure paths.
  • Founder-led execution: keep the product, qualification, and customer interpretation close to the person building the system.
