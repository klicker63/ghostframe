03 / Operating principles

Evidence over theater.

Originality matters. Real engineering matters more. The studio’s public claims should never outrun the systems behind them.

  1. 01

    Build the hard part first.

    Resolve the technical unknown that could kill the idea before polishing the story around it.

  2. 02

    Prove what actually works.

    Keep evidence attached to the claim, label the environment, and make the path to review legible.

  3. 03

    State limitations clearly.

    A prototype is a prototype. Research is research. The boundary is part of the product, not fine print.

  4. 04

    Kill weak ideas.

    Do not preserve a concept merely because effort has already been spent. Go deep only when the idea survives.
