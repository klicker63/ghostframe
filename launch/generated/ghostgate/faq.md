# FAQ

## Is the readiness check a certification?
No. It is a self-assessment and does not guarantee safety or replace independent testing.

## Does completing it send assessment answers to GhostFrame Studios?
No. The assessment, scoring, result, download, and Permission Envelope generation run locally in the browser. Only a separately submitted optional contact form sends bounded lead details.

## What is a Permission Envelope?
It is a structured release boundary covering allowed tools, prohibited operations, identities, data, approvals, systems, execution limits, logging, and invalidation triggers.

## What happens when the agent changes?
Material changes to behavior-shaping components can invalidate earlier evidence and require a new review or assessment.
