# Founder announcement

I am releasing GhostGate Agent Release Readiness Check, a free way to examine an AI agent’s release boundary before production.

Teams can give AI agents consequential tools and permissions without a version-bound record of what was tested, what was allowed, and who approved release.

The tool asks 15 focused control questions and generates a sample Permission Envelope locally in your browser. No login is required, and completing it does not send the answers to us.

This is deliberately a self-assessment—not certification, a safety guarantee, or a substitute for independent testing. The goal is to help a team reach a clearer, testable boundary.

Try it: https://www.ghostframestudios.com/tools/agent-release-readiness/?utm_source=ghostframe&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=ghostgate-launch-v01&utm_content=social

If you are responsible for an agent release, I would value blunt feedback about what the tool misses.
