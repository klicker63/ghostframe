# Founder announcement

I built GhostGate Agent Release Readiness Check, a free way to examine an AI agent’s release boundary before production.

Teams can give AI agents consequential tools and permissions without a version-bound record of what was tested, what was allowed, and who approved release.

The tool asks 15 focused control questions and generates a sample Permission Envelope locally in the browser. No login is required, and assessment answers are not sent to GhostFrame.

This is deliberately a self-assessment—not certification, a safety guarantee, or a substitute for independent testing. The goal is to help a team reach a clearer, testable boundary.

The useful rule behind it is simple: when the model, instructions, tools, permissions, memory, or deployment changes materially, treat the earlier release evidence as stale until the new boundary is reviewed.
