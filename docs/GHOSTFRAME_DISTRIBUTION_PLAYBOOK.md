# GhostFrame Zero-Paid-Ad Distribution Playbook

## Operating principle

Distribution begins with a useful artifact, a specific audience problem, and an honest invitation to inspect or respond. The first conversion path is:

`useful public explanation → free readiness tool → local result → optional qualified lead → scoped paid Release Check`

The free tool must stand alone. A visitor can finish, copy the Permission Envelope, download the result, and leave without signup.

## Channel system

### Free tools as acquisition

Teach a concrete release-control model, let the user apply it without surrendering data, and place the commercial offer after the result. Improve the tool from repeated confusion and objections, not vanity traffic.

### Founder-led distribution

Publish from a real founder identity. Explain the design choices, limits, and evidence. Reply personally to technical objections. Keep a lightweight record of question, audience, source, response, and lesson.

### Technical articles and search-focused content

Build durable pages around specific queries: AI agent permission boundaries, approval gates, immutable agent versions, prompt injection through tools, browser-agent credentials, agent evidence, and release checklists. Each article should contain an original control model, concrete example, honest limit, and one relevant CTA. Update articles when product behavior changes.

### Product Hunt

Prepare assets and maker answers after soft-launch feedback. Ask real users for feedback, never for a scripted positive review. On launch day, answer questions and disclose the paid offer. Measure qualified tool completions and conversations, not ranking alone.

### Show HN

Lead with what was built, how scoring works, what stays local, and where technical critique is wanted. Be available for detailed discussion. Do not repost if the submission receives little attention; use feedback elsewhere.

### Reddit

Research each subreddit’s current rules and contribution norms. Contribute useful comments before posting. Write a subreddit-specific explanation that works without clicking. Disclose the maker relationship and ask moderators when uncertain. Never coordinate votes or repost identical promotional copy.

### GitHub Discussions

Use only repositories or communities where a release-control checklist is relevant and external resources are allowed. Share the method or a reusable example, not a drive-by link. In GhostFrame-owned spaces, invite issue-level feedback on question wording and envelope fields.

### Discord and Slack communities

Ask moderators for permission. Prefer a short question, office-hour offer, or useful teardown. Do not automate posts or unsolicited DMs. Follow the community’s channel and cadence rules.

### DEV and technical publishing

Publish a complete technical article with canonical-link discipline. Explain algorithms and boundaries; do not disguise product copy as education. Repurpose diagrams and examples after the original article is stable.

### Podcasts and newsletters

Pitch a specific audience fit and an editorial angle, not a generic founder biography. Offer original examples or a short contribution. One respectful follow-up is the limit unless invited.

### Direct founder email

Contact only a verified, relevant business address published for professional contact or supplied through a relationship. Cite the public product/release detail that makes the note relevant. Never use scraped or guessed addresses. Send one initial note and, at most, one three-business-day follow-up.

### Agency and implementation partners

Identify agent builders, AI consultancies, security firms, and enterprise integrators whose clients face a genuine pre-release evidence gap. Start with joint control education or a referral conversation. Document conflicts and do not imply endorsement before a written agreement.

### Referral loops

In v0.1, referral is a manual learning loop: ask a satisfied tool user whether another release owner would benefit. Do not offer undisclosed compensation or implement tracking. Future referral software requires consent, attribution rules, fraud controls, disclosure, and privacy review.

### Fictional case studies and real case studies

Label fictional scenarios prominently and use them to teach a control failure. Real case studies require permission, verified evidence, scope limitations, and approval of public claims. Never pretend to be a customer.

## Launch sequence

1. Validate the product and claims.
2. Soft-launch the tool to a small technical group.
3. Fix material clarity and usability issues.
4. Publish one deep technical article.
5. Approach communities separately according to their rules.
6. Launch on Product Hunt and Show HN on different days.
7. Conduct narrow, researched founder and partner outreach.
8. Repurpose proven explanations into demos and shorter posts.
9. Review conversion and objections weekly.
10. Move durable insights back into the product, FAQ, templates, and content queue.

## Follow-up rules

- Reply while the context is current and add substance.
- Never argue with a moderator or evade removal.
- For direct outreach, send no more than one follow-up unless the recipient replies.
- Stop immediately after an opt-out or disinterest signal.
- Do not reuse private replies as marketing proof without permission.
- Separate a helpful answer from a sales request when possible.

## Community conduct and spam prevention

GhostFrame does not use automated mass posting, fake accounts, fake reviews, purchased engagement, vote coordination, community-rule violations, automated unsolicited DMs, scraped or guessed email addresses, AI-generated spam, undisclosed affiliate promotion, or invented customer identities. Templates are starting drafts only; a human must research, adapt, review, and choose whether to send each item.

## Measurement

Review the funnel by channel and campaign:

- relevant impressions or thread views when available;
- readiness starts;
- question progression and completion rate;
- result category distribution at aggregate level only;
- Permission Envelope copies and result downloads;
- paid Release Check CTA clicks;
- optional lead starts and successful submissions;
- qualified conversations;
- accepted scope reviews;
- objections, missing controls, and content questions;
- time invested per qualified conversation.

Do not collect assessment answers for attribution. Do not treat raw traffic, social reactions, or Product Hunt rank as proof of buyer demand.

## Free-to-paid conversion

The paid offer should appear when the user understands the gaps and exact release boundary. The CTA must explain the difference: the free tool is a self-assessment; the $5,000 Agent Release Check is an independent, fixed-scope evaluation of one exact version and tested configuration, with human-reviewed evidence. Qualification remains manual, and submitting contact information does not promise acceptance.
