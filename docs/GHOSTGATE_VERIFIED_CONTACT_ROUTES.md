## Verification standard

- **Identity:** a first-party company page or current accelerator/company record must support the person and role.
- **What the agent product does / technical fit:** the product must operate an agent or agent infrastructure with meaningful tools, permissions, external actions, or release-state changes.
- **Release or enterprise trigger:** a current product, security, release, or enterprise signal must make outreach timely.
- **Budget plausibility:** funding, revenue, enterprise pricing, or customer adoption may support plausibility; none is treated as proof of willingness to spend $5,000.
- **Accessibility:** the exact free route must be public. Inferred email patterns, generic support/sales funnels, GitHub issues, and communities that prohibit promotion do not pass.
- **Route labels:** **Direct** reaches the named person or a founder-owned inbox; **Permission-based** permits a narrowly scoped first message before a commercial follow-up; **Referral-only** can only route the sender to an appropriate owner.
