# GhostGate 30-Day Zero-Paid-Ad Launch Calendar

This schedule deliberately separates major channels. Owners should adjust dates for moderator permission, editorial timing, and product readiness; do not force a post because a calendar row exists.

| Day | Focus | Action and deliverable | Decision metric |
| ---: | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Baseline | Validate manifest, generate drafts, run product/tests/build, and record baseline funnel checks. | All gates pass; claims reviewed |
| 2 | Soft launch | Invite 3–5 trusted technical reviewers to complete the tool without coaching. | Completion blockers and wording notes |
| 3 | Feedback | Interview two reviewers about the highest-priority gap and envelope usefulness. | Repeated confusion identified |
| 4 | Fix | Resolve material accessibility, scoring explanation, or copy issues; rerun validation. | No new critical defects |
| 5 | Demo | Record a captioned 90-second walkthrough using a fictional agent and non-sensitive data. | Clear at mobile and desktop size |
| 6 | Technical article | Draft “An AI agent release is a versioned permission decision.” | Evidence and diagrams identified |
| 7 | Weekly review | Review starts, completions, drop-off, CTA clicks, and qualitative notes. | One prioritized product change |
| 8 | DEV preparation | Edit the technical article for DEV; verify canonical link and disclosure. | Human editorial review complete |
| 9 | Publish article | Publish the full technical article; answer substantive responses. | Engaged reads and tool starts |
| 10 | Reddit research | Read current rules and recent posts in 3 relevant communities; make useful non-promotional contributions. | One plausible community fit |
| 11 | Product Hunt prep | Finalize listing, screenshots, first comment, FAQ, and founder availability plan. | Listing preview reviewed |
| 12 | Founder outreach | Send up to 5 individually researched notes to verified, relevant contacts. | Replies and objections, not volume |
| 13 | Show HN prep | Adapt the technical build story; prepare direct answers on privacy and scoring. | Draft passes technical review |
| 14 | Weekly review | Compare content source to readiness starts and completions; update FAQ. | Best learning documented |
| 15 | Community permission | Ask moderators in one relevant Slack or Discord community for permission to share a value-first teardown. | Explicit permission or no post |
| 16 | Product Hunt launch | Publish Product Hunt listing and respond as the maker. Do not solicit scripted votes/reviews. | Qualified completions and questions |
| 17 | Product Hunt follow-up | Answer remaining questions, thank reviewers, and log objections. | Objection themes captured |
| 18 | Founder follow-up | Send the single three-business-day follow-up where relevant; close non-responsive threads. | Qualified replies |
| 19 | Product demo content | Publish a short clip on version invalidation with a link to the free tool. | Tool starts from demo UTM |
| 20 | Reddit contribution | Publish one rules-compliant, value-first post only if research supports it. | Discussion quality and saves |
| 21 | Weekly review | Check funnel health, endpoint errors, console errors, and lead quality. | Product/content change selected |
| 22 | Show HN launch | Submit the build-focused Show HN post and stay available for technical discussion. | Technical feedback and completions |
| 23 | Show HN follow-up | Convert recurring questions into FAQ or documentation changes. | FAQ change shipped |
| 24 | Partner research | Identify 5 relevant agent builders, agencies, or security integrators with a documented fit reason. | Five verified candidates |
| 25 | Partner outreach | Send up to 3 individualized partnership notes focused on client release controls. | Relevant conversations |
| 26 | Newsletter pitch | Pitch one carefully selected newsletter with a specific reader-use case. | Editor response or lesson |
| 27 | Podcast pitch | Pitch one show with three specific discussion angles and episode relevance. | Host response or lesson |
| 28 | Weekly review | Compare channels on qualified completions, CTA clicks, leads, conversations, and founder time. | Continue/stop decisions |
| 29 | Search backlog | Turn the strongest question into the next search-focused article and supporting demo. | Brief, keyword, evidence ready |
| 30 | Closeout | Publish a transparent founder reflection, update manifest/templates, and set the next 30-day cadence. | Documented lessons and next tests |

## Weekly review questions

1. Which source produced completed assessments rather than visits?
2. Which question or section caused confusion or abandonment?
3. Which result gaps led to a qualified commercial conversation?
4. What claim, screenshot, or example needs stronger evidence?
5. Which channel earned continued participation, and which should pause?
6. What did GhostFrame learn that belongs in the product rather than more promotion?
