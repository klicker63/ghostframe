01 / Problem

Security teams inherit claims.
They still have to make the decision.

AI systems accumulate authority, generate confident narratives, and reuse knowledge that can be wrong. Conventional controls rarely connect these risks into one evidence path.

The missing layer is not more confidence. It is reviewable evidence at the moment a consequential decision must be made.
