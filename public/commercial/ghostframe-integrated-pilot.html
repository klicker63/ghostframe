GhostFrame builds evidence-driven security systems for decisions that cannot rely on assumptions.

This integrated pilot gives security teams evidence across three moments: before operation, during an incident, and after contaminated AI knowledge has influenced behavior.

01 / BEFORE CONTROL

GhostGate

Qualify the agent before trust becomes access.

Evaluate an exact agent version against bounded behaviors, permissions, policy, and release conditions before higher-impact access is granted.

  • Version-bound qualification record
  • Human approval and explicit conditions
  • Evidence package for the release decision
02 / DURING VERIFY

Proofline

Know what they proved. Not what they claimed.

Turn incident assertions into a reviewable chain of evidence so responders can separate demonstrated facts from narrative confidence.

  • Claim-to-evidence traceability
  • Gaps, conflicts, and assumptions surfaced
  • Decision-ready incident record
03 / AFTER RESEARCH PREVIEW

Recall

What did corrupted AI knowledge affect, and can we repair it?

In a controlled setting, investigate how contaminated knowledge may propagate into retrieval, decisions, and downstream behavior.

  • Bounded influence tracing
  • Candidate repair paths for review
  • Human-controlled validation exercises
