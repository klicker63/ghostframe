07

Scope and limitations

This sample reports factual municipal-record research only for the named sources, identifiers, parcel binding, and timestamps. It does not provide a title search, title-insurance product, legal opinion, appraisal, survey, environmental review, inspection, zoning verification letter, code-compliance certification, or closing guarantee.

A “no matching record located” result is used only after a precisely scoped source search completes. Pending, inaccessible, paid, excluded, or human-review items remain distinct and visible.
