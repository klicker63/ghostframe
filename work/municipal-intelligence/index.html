03 / Evidence standard

No black-box score / No false certainty

The report shows what we know—and what we still don’t.

GhostFrame does not turn missing access into a reassuring result. A blocked source is not treated as “no records.” A pending government response stays pending. A no-match result means only that the named source was successfully searched for that property and time.

07 / Expected timing

Targets, with government timing kept honest.

GhostFrame targets a coverage and quote response within 1 business day and preliminary research within 2 business days after confirmed payment. While required external work remains pending, the target update cadence is every 2 business days. Final delivery is targeted within 1 business day after the final required source arrives and quality review passes.

Government and delegated providers control their response times. Their timing is not guaranteed by GhostFrame, and an open response stays open in the report.

09 / Useful boundaries

Municipal research—not a closing instrument.

GhostFrame Municipal Property Risk Reports provide factual municipal-record research for the included sources and search period.

They are not title searches, title commitments, title insurance, legal opinions, surveys, inspections, appraisals, zoning verification letters, code-compliance certificates, or closing guarantees.

Use the appropriate title, legal, inspection, survey, and other property professionals for decisions outside this report’s factual source scope.
