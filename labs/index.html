02 / Lab policy

No graduation by marketing.

  1. 01 / Name the unknown. A lab begins with a concrete technical question, not a category trend.
  2. 02 / Build the smallest honest test. The point is to expose the difficult assumption early.
  3. 03 / Preserve the boundary. Prototype, research, validated behavior, and planned capability stay visibly separate.
  4. 04 / Let weak ideas die. A discontinued experiment is a useful result when it prevents years of theater.
  5. 05 / Go deep when the signal survives. Only durable ideas earn the time required to become systems.

A relevant problem?

Give the experiment something real to collide with.

GhostFrame considers bounded technical collaborations where the problem is unusual, the scope can be controlled, and both sides are precise about what has and has not been proven.
